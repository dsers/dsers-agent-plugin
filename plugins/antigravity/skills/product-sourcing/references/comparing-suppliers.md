# Comparing suppliers

## What to compare

For each candidate, from DSers:

- **Same item?** Material, size, features, and variants match the reference product. Compare the images and the variant options.
- **Unit cost** of the variant the seller will sell most, and the range across variants. Costs differ between variants more often than sellers expect.
- **Shipping to the market**: method, cost, delivery time, and whether it has tracking that shows delivery in the customer's country.
- **Landed cost** = unit cost + shipping + any duties or fees the seller pays per parcel. Since August 2025, duties can apply to every parcel entering the US; ask the supplier whether a price includes them (see [rules.md](rules.md)).
- **Stock** for each variant the seller will sell.
- **Where it ships from**: a local warehouse is faster but usually costs more.
- **Track record**, when DSers returns it: store rating, order count, reviews.

From the user, or as a labeled estimate:

- **Selling price**, to show margin: (price − landed cost) ÷ price. If the user has no price yet, ask for a target margin and show the price that reaches it: (landed cost + fixed payment fee) ÷ (1 − target margin − payment fee rate), before ad costs. Use the user's payment fee, or an example rate labeled as an example.

## The comparison table

One row per candidate: supplier platform, product (as a link), unit cost, shipping method, shipping cost, delivery time, landed cost, stock, rating or orders, margin at the user's price. Mark values DSers did not return as unknown. Keep every amount in its currency; do not convert unless DSers returned a converted amount.

Below the table, recommend one candidate with the reason and the trade-off, for example "cheapest landed cost, but about a week slower than B". Name a backup with matching variants, so the product can keep selling if the first one runs out; the supplier-mapping skill can set it as a backup supplier after the product is in the store.

## Warning signs

- A price far below every other seller.
- No orders or reviews.
- Photos copied from another brand, branded or trademarked goods, or a brand name in the title.
- Very long delivery times, or shipping without tracking.
- Variants with very different costs under the same headline price.

## Before selling

- Recommend ordering a sample before advertising.
- Products that need extra checks (see [rules.md](rules.md)):
  - Children's products for the US need a Children's Product Certificate.
  - Products sold to EU consumers need a manufacturer, and an EU responsible person when the manufacturer is outside the EU, named on the listing.
  - Batteries, liquids, powders, and magnets have shipping restrictions; check the method allows them.
